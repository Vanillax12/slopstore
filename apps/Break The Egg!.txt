Break The Egg is a remake of an old scratch game made by me years ago, on my old account GameStartTree. (new one Tearquiet)
The original game had bad grammar and badly designed content, and was called Broke The Egg.
Using turbowarp and some other tools I have made a remake of this game, which has hyperrealistic graphics included for the funnies, better design, and more light and dark mode.
It also comes with better achievements tab and better click counter.

This is just your average clicker game. Enjoy!
